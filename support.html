Common questions

It isn't detecting anything when I play

Check that microphone access is granted: Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → Key Master. If it's on and still nothing registers, make sure no other app is holding the microphone, and that your device isn't routing audio to a headset without a mic. Playing a little louder or closer to the device also helps — the detector needs a clear fundamental pitch.

The key it names isn't what I expected

Key detection works from the notes it actually hears, so it needs a reasonable sample. A couple of notes isn't enough to distinguish, say, C major from A minor — they contain the same pitches. Play a phrase of eight seconds or so, ideally one that resolves, and it will have far more to go on. Ambiguity between relative keys is a genuine property of the music, not a fault in the reading.

The tuner jumps around

That's usually the note decaying or a room reflection. Pick the string cleanly and let it ring; read the needle in the first second or two while the tone is strong. If you're tuning a low string, be aware that some rooms reinforce low frequencies enough to confuse any tuner.

Can I use it without a guitar?

Yes. Hum, whistle, or play a melody from another device's speaker — the app responds to any sustained pitch. The lessons, fretboard charts and ear training work with no instrument at all.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Everything runs on the device. The only links that go online are the optional gear recommendations in the setup section, which open in your browser.

Where is my practice data stored, and how do I clear it?

On your device only — see the privacy policy. Deleting the app removes all of it, including any recordings you've made.

My recordings are taking up space

Recordings are saved locally and are not capped, so a long history of them can add up. You can delete individual recordings inside the app.

Reporting a bug or asking for a feature

Email is the fastest route. The most useful bug reports say what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead — plus your device and iOS version. Feature requests are genuinely welcome; a lot of what's in the app came from people asking.
