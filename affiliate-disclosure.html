Affiliate Disclosure

Key Master for iOS

Last updated 28 July 2026.

Current status

As of the date above, Key Master is not enrolled in any affiliate program, and the gear links in the app earn nothing. They are ordinary product links. If that changes, this page and the in-app notice will be updated to say so before the version that includes tracking is released.

What the gear links are

The instrument-setup section of the app recommends a small number of physical accessories — capos and setup tools such as a feeler gauge and a string-action ruler — with links out to retailers where you can buy them. They are there because they are the items actually needed to follow that part of the lessons.

How affiliate links would work, if enabled

If Key Master later joins a retailer's affiliate program, some of those links would become affiliate links. That means that if you buy something after following one, the app earns a small commission from the retailer.

Two things stay true if that happens:

  • It costs you nothing extra. The price is the same as going to the retailer directly. The commission comes out of the retailer's margin, not your pocket.
  • It does not change the recommendations. The items suggested are the ones suited to the job. A product does not get recommended, or recommended more prominently, because it pays better — and buying elsewhere, or not at all, is completely fine.

When any link in the app is a tracked affiliate link, the app itself displays a disclosure next to those recommendations. That notice is shown only when a commission is genuinely being earned — telling you a link is an affiliate link when it earns nothing would not be true.

Independence

No retailer or manufacturer has any editorial input into the app's lessons or recommendations, and none has paid for placement.
