Key Master

Play something. It tells you what key you're in.

Key Master listens to your guitar through your device's microphone and works out the key and scale you're playing in — then shows you the fretboard shapes that fit it. It also includes a tuner, ear training, and a set of lessons that go from "what is an interval" through to setting up your instrument.

Everything happens on your device. Audio is analysed locally and never uploaded. The app has no accounts, no analytics, and makes no network requests. See the privacy policy for the specifics.

What's in it

  • Key & scale detectionPlay or hum a passage and Key Master names the key, the scale, and the mode it's being played in.
  • TunerChromatic mode for any pitch, or string mode for guitar tunings, with an adjustable A4 reference.
  • Ear trainingIntervals, chords, scale degrees and progressions, at three difficulty levels, with quizzes and streaks.
  • LessonsBasics, scales, chords, fretboard shapes, a guided course, and a section on setting up your guitar.
  • Fretboard chartsFull-neck diagrams for scales and chord shapes, including pentatonics, viewable full screen.
  • Practice trackingDaily goals, streaks, per-mode accuracy and achievements, stored only on your device.

Requirements

iOS 17.0 or later. Runs on iPhone and iPad. Microphone access is required for pitch detection, tuning, and key detection — that is the core of what the app does. You can decline it and still use the lessons and fretboard charts.

Free

Key Master is free to download with no ads and no subscription.
