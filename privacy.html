Privacy Policy

Key Master for iOS

Effective 28 July 2026. This policy covers the Key Master iOS application.

The short version: Key Master collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers. Your audio is analysed on your device and never leaves it. Nothing you do in the app is transmitted anywhere.

Information we collect

None. Key Master does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. The app has no backend service to send data to, no user accounts, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no advertising or tracking frameworks of any kind. It performs no tracking as defined by Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, and it contacts no tracking domains.

Microphone

Key Master requests microphone access because listening to your instrument is the central function of the app — it is how the tuner, the pitch detection, and the key and scale detection work.

Audio from the microphone is analysed in real time, entirely on your device. It is not uploaded, streamed, or sent to any server, and it is not used for any purpose other than detecting pitch and musical key while you are using the relevant feature. Microphone access is used only while the app is in the foreground and you are on a screen that listens.

You may decline microphone access, or revoke it later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone. The lessons, fretboard charts, and other non-listening features continue to work without it.

Recordings

If you choose to record a passage, that recording is saved as an audio file in the app's own private storage on your device. Recordings are not uploaded anywhere.

The only time a recording leaves your device is if you explicitly share it yourself using the iOS share sheet. At that point it goes wherever you send it, and is governed by whatever service you chose. You can delete recordings from within the app at any time.

Data stored on your device

Key Master saves the following locally, using Apple's standard app-preferences storage, so that your progress persists between launches:

  • Your app settings, such as tuning choice and reference pitch
  • Practice statistics — answers, accuracy, daily goals, streaks and achievements
  • Any recordings you have made

All of this is stored only in the app's private container on your device. It is not backed up to any service we control. Deleting the app removes all of it permanently.

Links to other websites

The instrument-setup section of the app includes recommendations for physical accessories, such as capos and setup tools, with links that open in your browser to third-party retailers. We do not share any information about you with those retailers — following a link tells them only what your browser tells any website you visit. Once you leave the app, that retailer's own privacy policy applies, not this one. See our affiliate disclosure for how those links work commercially.

Children

Key Master is suitable for all ages and contains no age-restricted content. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Your rights

Regulations such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export, or delete personal data a company holds about you. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, or delete. The data described above never reaches us — it stays on your device, under your control, and deleting the app erases it.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date above revised before that version ships.
